FRAME 90-DEGREE JOINTS:
Flat 2x4 frame: use approximately 5 to 5-1/8 in structural wood screws
through the 3.5 in-deep front/rear rail into the member end.
Use two screws per joint where practical. Predrill.
Wood glue may supplement this connection.

POST CONNECTIONS:
Use approximately 3 to 3-1/8 in structural wood screws through the
1.5 in-thick flat upper/lower frame into each vertical post.
Primary top fastening for front posts passes through the upper front-to-back member
into the post, not through the upper front rail near its rear edge.
Wood glue may supplement both connection types.
Do not use drywall screws. Metal angle brackets are not required.
